Overview & Student Journey

Welcome to UGSkill: A physical academy campus workflow under the hood

The Physical Academy Metaphor

Think of UGSkill not as a cold set of files, but as a vibrant physical career-focused campus. Every feature you see on the screen represents a physical space or person working to help students get jobs:

📚

The Classroom (LMS)

Where students attend lectures and complete timestamped quizzes, managed by the LMS controller.

🕵️

The Exam Hall

A highly secure room with proctors checking webcams and browser tabs to ensure no cheating happens during tests.

🤝

The Placements Desk

The matching desk that checks student eligibility (like grades or resume uploads) and organizes interview rooms.

💡
Steering your AI Assistant

When instructing AI, rather than asking for "a page that updates user data," describe the specific room they are entering: "We need to update the student registration checklist at the Placements Desk." This aligns code structure with the business purpose.
